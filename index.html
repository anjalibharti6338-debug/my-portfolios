About me

Designer at heart, systems thinker by nature

I'm Anjali Bharti, a product and UI designer based in San Francisco. I specialize in designing digital products that balance business goals with genuine human needs.

Previously at Stripe, Figma, and a handful of early-stage startups. I bring a structured, research-first approach to every project — but I'm equally comfortable rolling up my sleeves in a fast-moving team.

When I'm not designing, I'm probably teaching workshops on design systems, hiking somewhere with bad cell service, or overcooking pasta.

How I work

My design process

01
Discover
User interviews, competitive audits, and stakeholder alignment to understand the real problem before touching a frame.
02
Define
Synthesis, jobs-to-be-done mapping, and clear success metrics that keep the team focused throughout the project.
03
Design
Lo-fi explorations → tested prototypes → pixel-perfect handoff. I design in the open, sharing early and iterating fast.
04
Deliver
Annotated specs, design tokens, and close collaboration with engineers through launch — and beyond.
